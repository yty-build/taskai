# taskai

**One terminal. One task. Your best AI agents, working together.**

TaskAI turns the terminal into a calm command center for ambitious work.
You speak to one Manager; the Manager brings in the right agents, keeps their work organized, and returns one clear answer.

## Open it and start

Launch `taskai` and land on your Tasks.
Create a task, choose the AI you want as Manager, and begin typing.
There is no maze of setup screens and no need to manage a wall of terminals.

## A workspace you can understand at a glance

```text
┌─ Launch the product ─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Sessions                         │ Manager                    │
│                                  │                            │
│ ● Lead · Claude                  │ › Evaluate this repository │
│   ◐ Architecture · Codex         │                            │
│   ✓ Research · Grok              │ I’m reviewing it with two  │
│   ● Implementation · Codex       │ specialists now…           │
│                                  │                            │
├──────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────┤
│ Manager working · 3 specialists             Type a message › │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Your Managers and their specialists stay visible on the left.
The conversation stays focused on the right.
Progress, interruptions, and completed work are obvious without exposing internal noise.

## Designed for the keyboard

Every screen shows the keys that work there.
`Enter` opens or confirms, `Esc` takes you back, and one clear input area is always visible when you can speak.
You can create, switch, interrupt, leave, and return without losing your place.

## Work that survives the terminal

Close the window. Detach. Come back tomorrow.
Your tasks and agent conversations resume where they stopped, ready for the next instruction.

TaskAI makes multi-agent work feel less like operating infrastructure—and more like leading a great team.
